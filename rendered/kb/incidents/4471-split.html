  • parser. Every tenant on file writes the day first, and the shared parser reads the month first. Any date with a day of 12 or less comes out swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July.
  • hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result is the same swap through a different bug, and only on the booking form of booking-v3 tenants. A parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day. These reports describe a day lost or gained, not a swap: Tuesday 12 May shown as Monday 11 May, a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto seeing different days for the same meeting, every June booking one day off. That points to time zones or offsets. The desk already marked these ids as one-day moves in desk-notes.json, but they were still counted as parser.

How they ended up in one ticket

Triage merges by subject line alone (decision 0001). “Wrong date again” is the same subject whatever the cause, so all three problems landed in #4471. Intake’s tag doesn’t help either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the cause on dozens of these reports. The desk’s own cause table on #4471 already sets the form reports apart, and the one-day reports are already in the desk’s notes. Nobody opened tickets for them.

The export repeats some ids across overlapping pages. Count unique ids, not rows.

What to do

  1. Keep #4471 for the parser problem only. Move the hint and one-day reports to their tickets.
  2. Before you merge a date report, check the tenant’s booking form and whether the report describes a swap or a single-day shift. The subject line is not enough.
